1 · Mechanisms — 四类机制分类(顶层 taxonomy)

调控机制按以下四类组织(mechanism category)。各诊断轴(axis)是类别内的具体实现,不是独立的一级类别。

Category定义Diagnostic axes(类内实例)
M1 · Direct cis-regulatory DNATF 直接占据启动子/增强子等顺式元件,经转录机器或辅因子调控靶基因acute TF degradation(ZNF143 / BCL11A / RUNX1 / PAX3-FOXO1 / TRPS1 / TAL1 等);Mediator(MED14)与 BET reader(BRD4)辅因子轴
M2 · Chromatin-mediated调控效应依赖染色质可及性、核小体重塑或组蛋白/DNA 修饰chromatin gating;remodeler 轴(SMARCA5 / BRG1);组蛋白修饰轴(H3K27ac,八因子)
M3 · Combinatorial TF须与另一 TF 互作方能产生调控效应互作对组:CTCF–RAD21、BRD4–CDK7、CDK7–POLR2A(已建);OCT4–SOX2–NANOG、YAP–AP-1/TEAD(文献组)
M4 · Regulatory ncRNA-mediatedmiRNA、lncRNA 或 eRNA 作为 TF 与靶基因间的必要中间体eRNA 轴(Gorbovytska 2022,PEARL 2021);miRNA 轴(miR-155、miR-34a/p53)——单元构建中
示例说明:PRC2 与诊断窗口(mechanism-diagnostic 的 illustrative example)

PRC2 抑制是"机制诊断扰动"的经典示意:若边为 M2(锁被开),靶应响应;若为 M1,则不应响应。但 H3K27me3 更替慢于本基准的急性诊断窗(30 min–6 h),故 PRC2 目前不作为 acute benchmark 的数据点——这体现对实验设计边界的界定,而非遗漏。

2 · Diagnostic Perturbations — 方法核心

Mechanism-diagnostic perturbations are selected such that competing mechanistic explanations make distinguishable predictions about the target-gene response. For each regulatory edge, perturbations are curated from published literature and public datasets, prioritizing interventions with experimentally validated effects on the relevant TF, cis-regulatory element, chromatin state, TF–TF interaction, or regulatory RNA.

Where two candidate mechanisms (H1, H2) make opposing directional predictions, the observed target response is used to derive a mechanism label under a preregistered decision rule: direction-only (UP / DOWN / NO_CHANGE, magnitude-agnostic); a response satisfying neither hypothesis is marked inconclusive and retained, never forced. Labels are re-derived from deposited data and never copied from paper claims; the derived-versus-stated disagreement is itself reported.

(R, T, C) → (H1, H2) → P → YT → Label

3 · Benchmark Units

(TF, target gene, mechanism, context, perturbation, post-perturbation expression response)

每单元附审计字段:响应方向与效应量、decision rule、binding filter、confidence tier、accession(GEO/ENCODE)、quarantine 标记。数据仅取自同行评审期刊与公开存档;单元按 (TF, target, context, perturbation) 去重;争议案例单列隔离。
